Verdict

    New idea: does the market confuse luck with skill?

    For every team we track how it has done against what the bookmaker expected, then split that surprise into results, shot dominance, finishing luck (goals beyond what shots on target justify) and goalkeeper luck. The rules were written down and committed before the 2016–2026 holdout was run once.

    Effect on the next match, by part

    t-statistic of each part in predicting goal difference beyond the market. Beyond ±2 is significant. Negative means the market over-reacts.

    What the luck effect is worth

    Handicap bets on the holdout, re-priced at different bookmaker margins. A projection, not a test.

    Model vs market

    Ranked probability score (RPS) is the standard accuracy measure for 1X2 forecasts; lower is better. Bookmaker prices with the margin removed are the benchmark. The chart shows each model's gap to that benchmark on the holdout seasons.

    Strategies

    Six strategies fixed before looking at results. Flat 1-unit stakes. ROI is profit per unit staked; the range is a 95% bootstrap interval.

    Cumulative profit, holdout seasons

    Where the profit comes from

    Holdout ROI by the edge the strategy claimed. When profit sits only in the largest edges, the best price was usually a stale or outlier quote that is hard to get in practice.

    Overfitting check

    Every combination of model, price, edge threshold and odds cap, scored on the development window and then on the holdout. If development results predicted the future, the dots would rise to the right.

    Current ratings

    Latest Elo and pseudo-xG attack/defence for teams active in the last year, as percent above or below the division average. A higher defence figure means fewer goals conceded.

    Method

    Data
    football-data.co.uk results, shots and odds (Bet365, market best price; Pinnacle and closing prices when downloaded directly).
    Ratings
    Elo with goal-difference weighting and a self-adjusting home advantage; pi-ratings (Constantinou & Fenton 2013); online Dixon-Coles attack/defence on goals and on a pseudo-xG blend of goals and shots on target; shots-on-target ratings. Promoted and relegated teams are re-based using how earlier movers settled.
    Probabilities
    Ordered logit, multinomial logistic regression and gradient boosting on the rating features, re-trained every season on prior data only (walk-forward). The blend adds the bookmaker's own probabilities as inputs.
    Backtest
    One bet per match when model probability × price − 1 exceeds the edge threshold. Development window picks; holdout judges.
    Caveats
    Best-price odds assume you can always get the top price; soft bookmakers limit winning accounts; no closing-line check in the mirror data. Many strategies were tested, so a few will look good by chance.
